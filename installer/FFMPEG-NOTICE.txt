FFmpeg

clip-mcp uses FFmpeg to read, cut and render video. The installer does not carry FFmpeg: it
downloads the build below during installation, checks it against its SHA-256, and keeps
ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe in this folder for clip-mcp alone. Nothing is added to the PATH.
